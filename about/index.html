About

I’m Hamit Sehjal, a software engineer and indie founder.

I built HelloCelpip after struggling to find a realistic and affordable way to prepare for CELPIP myself. It became my first product and my first experience turning a personal problem into something other people would pay for.

This site is where I document that process honestly: what I build, why I make certain decisions, what goes wrong, and what I learn.
